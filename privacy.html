1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Jordan Tatue, an independent software developer established in France, reachable at tatuejordan@gmail.com.

An important distinction applies throughout this policy:

  • For data about you, the person or business who signs up and connects an account, I act as controller: I decide why and how it is processed.
  • For data about your own customers — the people who message your business account or place an order through it — I act as a processor on your behalf. You remain the controller of that data, you decide what happens to it, and I process it only on your documented instructions.

2. Scope

This policy covers the applications and integrations I publish under my own name, including those registered with the Meta and TikTok developer platforms. It does not cover Meta, TikTok, or any other third-party service you use directly: those are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Data obtained from Meta and TikTok

When you connect a Meta or TikTok account, the application receives only the permissions you approved on the platform's own consent screen. That data is used exclusively to deliver the features you asked for, and is handled in accordance with the Meta Platform Terms, the Meta Developer Policies and the TikTok Developer Terms of Service.

You can withdraw those permissions at any time, either inside the application or from the platform's own settings. Withdrawing them stops all further access immediately. See Data Deletion for what happens next.

5. Messaging and consent

Where an application helps you reply to conversations, it does not send unsolicited messages on its own initiative. Messages leave only when you send them, or when you configure an automated reply. You remain responsible for having a lawful basis to contact the people you message, and for complying with the messaging rules of each platform.

6. Who else can see the data

The data is not shared with anyone except the service providers strictly needed to run the application, each bound by a contract that limits them to processing it on my instructions:

  • hosting and database providers, to store the data;
  • error-monitoring providers, to receive technical logs;
  • the platforms themselves (Meta, TikTok), to carry out the actions you requested.

I will also disclose data where I am legally required to, for example in response to a valid order from a competent authority. Where the law allows it, I will tell you first.

7. Where the data is stored

Data is hosted in the European Union wherever the provider offers it. If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, that transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or by an adequacy decision.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, access tokens are stored encrypted, and access to production systems is restricted and authenticated. No system is perfectly secure, and I do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal data and is likely to result in a risk to your rights, I will notify you and the competent supervisory authority within the deadlines set by the GDPR.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask me to: access your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and receive it in a portable format. Where processing rests on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

Write to tatuejordan@gmail.com. I will answer within one month. I may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

If you are not satisfied with my answer, you may lodge a complaint with the French supervisory authority, the CNIL, or with the authority of the country where you live.

11. Deleting your data

You can have your data deleted at any time. The procedure, the deadlines and the exceptions are set out on a dedicated page: Data Deletion.

12. Children

These applications are intended for professional use and are not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child's data has reached the service, write to me and I will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top of this page is updated and, where the change is significant, I will tell you by email before it takes effect. Continuing to use the application after that date means the new version applies.
