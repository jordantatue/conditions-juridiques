Data Deletion

This page explains how to have your data erased from the applications published by Jordan Tatue, how long it takes, and what is kept afterwards. It is the data deletion instructions page referenced in the Meta and TikTok developer configurations for those applications.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the application

  1. Open the application and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then Account.
  3. Choose Disconnect account to revoke a single platform connection, or Delete my account to remove everything.
  4. Confirm. The deletion starts immediately.

Option 2 — Ask by email

Send a request to tatuejordan@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request, and include the email address or platform username linked to your account. I may ask you to confirm your identity before acting, so that nobody can delete your data on your behalf.

I acknowledge requests within 72 hours and complete them within 30 days, as required by the GDPR.

Option 3 — Revoke access from the platform itself

You can cut off access without going through me at all. This stops any further data from reaching the application, and triggers deletion of what was already received.

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → select the application → Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → select the application → Remove.
  • TikTok: Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions → select the application → Disconnect.

What gets deleted

  • your account identity and profile information;
  • all platform access and refresh tokens, which are revoked first;
  • the list of connected business accounts;
  • scheduled and published content stored by the application;
  • conversations retrieved on your behalf;
  • orders and delivery plans created in the application.

Backups holding your data are overwritten on a rolling 30-day cycle, so a copy may persist in an offline backup for up to 30 days after deletion. It is not restored into the live service and is not used for any purpose.

What is kept, and why

A small amount of data may be retained after deletion, strictly where the law requires or allows it:

DataWhyHow long
Invoices and accounting records, if you ever paid for the service French commercial and tax law 10 years
Minimal record of the deletion itself (date, account reference) To prove the request was honoured 3 years
Anonymised technical logs Security and abuse detection; no longer personal data 12 months
Content already published. Posts or messages that were already sent to Facebook, Instagram or TikTok live on those platforms, not in my systems. Deleting your data here does not remove them. You must delete them from the platform itself.

Confirmation

Once the deletion is complete, I send a written confirmation to the email address associated with the account, stating what was deleted and when.
