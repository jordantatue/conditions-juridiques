Who is behind these applications

Jordan Tatue, an independent software developer based in France. I design and operate small business automation tools that connect to social and messaging platforms on behalf of the businesses that use them.

Contact for any privacy, legal or data request: tatuejordan@gmail.com.

What the applications do

Depending on the application and the permissions you grant, they can:

  • schedule and publish content to social accounts you own or manage;
  • receive, display and help you reply to conversations sent to your business accounts;
  • turn those conversations into orders, and help you plan deliveries.

They act on your instructions and on your own accounts. They are built for professional and business use, not for personal consumer use.

Applications covered. These documents apply to every application I publish under my own name, including those registered with the Meta and TikTok developer platforms. Each application names this policy in its platform configuration.

Platform compliance

Where an application connects to Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp) or to TikTok, it is operated in line with those platforms' developer terms and data policies. Data obtained through their APIs is used only to provide the features you asked for. It is never sold, never used for advertising targeting, and never transferred to data brokers.
